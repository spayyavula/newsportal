Which chart library renders the structured exhibits?

All three render SVG, work with Next.js server components, and respect site typography. They differ in defaults, bundle size, and how much custom styling is needed to hit McKinsey-style clarity.

Recharts
~95KB gzipped · React-first · large community

A. Recharts

React-first declarative API. Largest community for editorial chart needs. Bundle: ~95KB gzipped. Needs more theming work to look truly McKinsey — defaults are generic. Easy to find examples and copy. Lowest risk choice.

Observable Plot
~55KB gzipped · D3 lineage · editorial defaults out of the box

B. Observable Plot

By Mike Bostock (D3 author). Editorial defaults out of the box — typography, baselines, axis treatment closer to FT/NYT/McKinsey style with less customization. Bundle: ~55KB. Smaller community, but the defaults often beat a fully-themed Recharts setup. Best fit for McKinsey style.

Visx
~varies (compose only what you use) · most flexible · most code per chart

C. Visx (by Airbnb)

Composable D3 primitives wrapped in React. Highest ceiling for custom design, but every chart requires more code. Bundle is what you import — can be smaller than Recharts. Skip unless we want pixel-perfect control over every exhibit.
